# 0002 We keep coining data.judaicalink.org URIs

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-10-07

## Context

`data.judaicalink.org` resolves to Frankfurt. Data developed in Mannheim (new entity pages, NLI records,
complementary datasets) needs URIs before Frankfurt has adopted it. Alternatives were a separate
development namespace (with renaming on adoption) or a fallback resolver between Frankfurt and Mannheim.

## Decision

- All URIs are coined in `http://data.judaicalink.org/data/<namespace>/…`, also for data that only exists
  in Mannheim so far. They are the final URIs.
- Until Frankfurt adopts a dataset, its new URIs do not resolve at data.judaicalink.org. This is accepted.
- Demonstrators on mannheim.judaicalink.org rewrite `data.judaicalink.org` URIs on the fly to their own
  views (e.g. `https://mannheim.judaicalink.org/entities/ep/<id>`), so that they can show their own
  representation of any resource.

## Consequences

- No renaming on adoption; the URI rules of [0009](0009-entity-pages.md) (never reuse a URI) apply to all
  URIs we coin.
- Namespaces must be chosen carefully before data is published (see the open issues on namespaces).
