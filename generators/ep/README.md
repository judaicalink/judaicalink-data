# ep: generator history

The legacy entity pages (2021), by Marco Rovera: one page per entity, pooling the records of all datasets, plus
labels, person and place classification, birth and death data and a subcategorisation. Superseded by
ep-2026-10-06 (generated by `ep_generator.py`, kg-complements), which starts from this set.

## History

- 2021-01-05: `ep_generator.md` (Jupytext notebook) and `ep_manager.py` (judaicalink-generators `e6686ff`).
- 2021-01-22: run_0 of the generator in Marco Rovera's working folder (log on the labs VM: 13:17–13:28); the
  working copy has two uncommitted edits (drop empty pages, log the triple count), without effect on a first run.
- 2021-02 … 05: selectors for labels (`286651f`, cleanup `c170edb`), persons and locations (`b3df5f7`, committed
  after the run), birth and death dates and places (`747e73c`), subcategorisation (`4865ffd`).
- 2025-06-05 ported to Python scripts and switched to a local endpoint (Benjamin Schnabel); 2025-11 moved to
  `legacy/entity-pages/`.

## Inputs

- The SPARQL endpoint of 2021 (`data.judaicalink.org/sparql`, all graphs as loaded then): no snapshot.
- `ep_index.pickle` (1,717,191 pages): not in the dumps; on the labs VM and in the private backup.

## Reproduction

`entity_pages` and `subcategorization` are **reproducible from the intermediates** (backup); the selectors are
**not** (live endpoint, result order). Page numbering cannot be reproduced even with the same endpoint state
(hash-randomised set order). Known defect: every IRI of every graph became a page, including about 750,000 cm-tagme
references and 362,000 compact-memory pages (singleton pages).
