# ep: generator history

The legacy entity pages (2021), by Marco Rovera: one page per entity, pooling the records of all datasets, plus
labels, person and place classification, birth and death data and a subcategorisation. The framework generator
(below) makes the next versions; the earlier attempt `ep-2026-10-06` (kg-complements `ep_generator.py`, never
published) is not carried over (decision 0022).

## Inputs

- The SPARQL endpoint of 2021 (`data.judaicalink.org/sparql`, all graphs as loaded then): no snapshot.
- `ep_index.pickle` (1,717,191 pages): not in the dumps; on the labs VM and in the private backup.

## Reproduction

`entity_pages` and `subcategorization` are **reproducible from the intermediates** (backup); the selectors are
**not** (live endpoint, result order). Page numbering cannot be reproduced even with the same endpoint state
(hash-randomised set order). Known defect: every IRI of every graph became a page, including about 750,000 cm-tagme
references and 362,000 compact-memory pages (singleton pages).

## Framework generator (2026-10-12)

`generate.py` makes a version of `ep` from the previous version and the stable versions of the datasets (decision
0022): a fresh closure of the identity links over the records, the comparison with the previous pages (kept, merged
into the oldest IRI, split, retired, new), the facts chosen by the precedence of 2026-10-06 (`ep_generator.py`, with
the dataset names of the catalogue, `jl:occupationURI`, organisations as a core class), two files (`ep.ttl.gz`, the
pages; `ep-history.ttl.gz`, the events). It stops when an IRI of the previous version is neither a page nor in the
history, when a dataset with records is not in the precedence list, or when two records known to be different
(`KNOWN_DISTINCT`, the conflations corrected on 2026-10-12) are in one group again. The run log lists the groups with
several Wikidata items or incompatible classes, keys shared by many records of one dataset, the splits and merges.
About two minutes, 8 GB of memory.

The first version (2026-10-12) is made from 2021-05-28: of its 1,717,191 IRIs, 246,291 stay pages, 14,917 are merged
into another page (among them the legacy NLI pages of the same person, now linked through `nli-records`), 1,492 are
split (1,481 legacy NLI URIs that stood for several people, followed through `prov:wasDerivedFrom` of `ls-nli-legacy`;
11 conflations of 2021, among them the two Abrahams, Hai and Sherira Gaon, Marseille and the composer Paul Marselle),
1,454,491 are retired (mentions, pages and images of Compact Memory and other IRIs that are no person, place,
organisation, work or subject); 63,923 new pages, numbers from 2,717,191 on (highest 2,781,113). 310,214 pages:
290,466 persons, 11,442 places, 7,312 works, 830 subjects, 164 organisations. The version was made again on
2026-10-12 (unpublished) with epidat 2026-10-12 (one record per Epidat person: 41,378 pages, 37 joined with other
records through Epidat's GND links), the YIVO articles (typed by record-classes, joined with other records through
ls-yivo-wikidata: 534 of 2,147 pages with YIVO records, decision 0025), geo-coordinates 2026-10-12 (`jl:Place`,
`data/geo-coordinates/`; the old IRIs are closure nodes), sosy and rujen-translation 2026-10-12, and again with footprints 2026-10-12 (its identifier links now IRIs:
708 of 11,532 pages with Footprints records joined with other records) and haskala 2026-10-12 (the whole database:
250 of 2,723 pages joined); classes stated by record-classes count for the dataset of the record (`CLASS_DATASETS`).
Nine class conflicts were source errors (Footprints and Haskala persons linked to the VIAF cluster of a work:
"Jerusalem Talmud" as a person, a Haskala person with the VIAF of Hegel's Phenomenology); corrected in
corrections-links 2026-10-12-2.

Found while porting (2026-10-12), fixed in the data, not here: bhr's links to the EntityFacts service page and its
empty GND numbers (new version of bhr), 2014links' links of rujen persons to places (new version of 2014links),
template links of gnd-persons-entityfacts, and 76 wrong links in nine datasets (corrections-links); two errors of the
key normalisation (DBpedia titles, hyphenated ISNIs) and the use of `prov:wasDerivedFrom` as an identity link were
errors of the port, corrected before the version. Compared with `ep-2026-10-06` (first made on 2026-10-12, before the YIVO and epidat records): 295,384 instead of 294,129 pages,
the legacy NLI pages carried over (no exception), splits and merges by comparison instead of growing clusters.

## Version 2026-10-26

Made from ep 2026-10-19 and the stable versions of 2026-10-26: footprints from the API (decision 0034) with
record-classes, the authority datasets and linksets of the cascade of 2026-10-26 (without the withdrawn 2014links),
and ls-footprints-viaf, the VIAF numbers of Footprints judged correct one by one (decision 0037). New rule: places
joined through a GeoNames key may hold several records of one dataset (decision 0036; 14 such joins, all of
footprints: a record of the city and one of its administrative unit, e.g. Breslau, Bad Homburg, Offenbach).

Result: 323,117 entities from 778,005 records; of the 313,312 pages of 2026-10-19, 311,034 keep their IRI, 1,009 are
merged, 260 split, 1,009 retired; 12,083 new IRIs (mostly the works, persons and places of Footprints). Merged: the
pages of the footprints IRIs of 2024 with the pages of the same persons and places, through ls-footprints-viaf and
the GeoNames ids (the oldest IRI is kept, sometimes the Footprints one). Split: the footprints IRIs of 2024 that stood
for several records (`dcterms:isReplacedBy` to each; a name hash for all persons of that name). Retired: 560 pages of
footprints IRIs of 2024 that match no record now (names changed since, the regions and countries of 2024), and 449
pages of authority records that only the seeds of 2014links had brought into the authority datasets (e.g. Americas,
Kramatorsk). 23,280 pages have Footprints records, 2,134 of them with authority records and 971 with records of other
datasets (NLI 527, geo-coordinates 376, ubffm 276, hhkeydocs 135, yivo 113). Refused as a second record of footprints:
23 links, where two Footprints persons share a VIAF cluster (duplicates in Footprints, or one link wrong).
Conflicts: 41 incompatible classes; several GND identifiers 1,526, LC 1,671, Wikidata 2,114, VIAF 8,303, GeoNames
639 (and 1,455 closely linked).

## Version 2026-10-19

Made from ep 2026-10-12 and the stable versions of 2026-10-19 (decisions 0029, 0031, 0032):

- **Identity only from our linksets** (`identity = linksets`, the default since then): ls-gnd, ls-lc, ls-geonames,
  ls-viaf, ls-wikidata, ls-dbpedia and the older linksets; each counts only the identity its datasets declare
  (catalogue field `identity`), and only for units above the quality threshold (`catalog/link-quality.toml`).
- **Members:** the records of the record datasets and of the authority datasets; a replaced IRI is never a member
  (586 typed IRIs); what a linkset types is no record; groups of authority records only get no page (2,941).
- **One record per record dataset:** a link that would bring a second record of the same record dataset into an
  entity is refused, in an order of trust (`KEY_ORDER`), and logged (788, mostly NLI duplicates); datasets may declare
  several records per entity (`several_per_entity`: geo-coordinates, haskala).
- **All identifiers, one value per fact:** every identifier is linked, a replaced one as its successor; names, dates,
  places, coordinates and abstracts come from the first record by a fixed order (`record_order`: dataset precedence,
  then not succeeded, not a pseudonym, a populated place before an administrative unit, named by more datasets, the
  lowest identifier).
- **Report:** several identifiers of one authority per entity, replaced identifiers, GeoNames features not connected
  through pairs of the same name within 25 km, incompatible classes.

Result: 313,312 entities from 767,626 records; of the 310,214 pages of 2026-10-12, 306,188 keep their IRI, 2,428
are merged, 1,395 split, 203 retired; 7,124 new IRIs. Splits mostly where links are now below the quality threshold
(footprints, 2014links: rujen and geo-coordinates records joined only through them) or refused as a second record of
one dataset (NLI duplicates). Conflicts: 41 incompatible classes; several GND identifiers 1,533, LC 1,668, Wikidata
2,152, VIAF 8,358, GeoNames 648 (and 1,452 closely linked).
