A label in English and German (`jl:label`) for every class and property used in the JudaicaLink datasets, for
interfaces and queries.

<!--more-->

The labels come from the dataset that coins a term, the JudaicaLink ontology, the term's own vocabulary, or Wikidata
for its properties.
